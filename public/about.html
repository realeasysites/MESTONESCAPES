Dan Morales-Walsh and the crew

More than half his life working with stone, and building Maine Stone Scapes since 2016.

Dan’s first exposure to stone was working with his dad. Years with larger landscaping companies in southern Maine taught him many ways to approach a project. But his passion has always been what can be done with two hands and a pile of stones.

  1. 2013Ship Cove, Fort Williams

    Dan started building at the beach with only the stone he found there, to learn the dynamics of dry stone structures. His sculptures grew more ambitious and were featured on 207 and in the Portland Press Herald.

  2. 2016Maine Stone Scapes

    In July 2016 he committed fully to his own business, the only way to take on the unique projects he wanted to build.

  3. 2018England, Scotland and Ontario

    Four weeks learning from some of the best dry stone wallers in West Yorkshire and Lancaster, then a week in Caithness, Scotland with master waller George Gunn. That May, in Holstein, Ontario, he passed the DSWA Level 2 certification test, skipping Level 1.

  4. 2020The first hire

    Dave joined as the first employee, and Dan completed the DSWA instructor certification course.

  5. TodayTeaching the craft

    Dan serves as Vice Chairman of The Stone Trust in Dummerston, Vermont, a nonprofit that preserves and advances dry stone walling. He teaches intro courses and certification prep around New England and is working to open an affiliated learning and certification site in Maine.
